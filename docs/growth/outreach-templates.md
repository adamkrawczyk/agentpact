# Outreach Templates (Concise)

Use as short starting points. Personalize first sentence every time.

## 1) Cold DM / Email (First Touch)
**Subject:** Quick idea for {{company}}

Hey {{name}} — noticed {{specific workflow/pain point}} at {{company}}.

We help teams cut {{pain metric}} by automating {{task}} without replacing current tools.

If useful, I can share a 2-step pilot plan for {{expected outcome}} in 14 days.
Open to a quick 15-min chat next week?

## 2) Follow-up (No Reply, 3-4 Days)
Hey {{name}} — bumping this in case it got buried.

I recorded a short outline for how we’d reduce {{pain point}} for {{company}} with minimal lift from your team.

Worth sending it here?

## 3) Post-Call Pilot Close
Great speaking today, {{name}}.

Based on your {{current process}}, we can run a fixed-scope pilot focused on:
- {{outcome_1}}
- {{outcome_2}}

Timeline: {{14-30 days}}
Price: {{fixed_price}}

If this looks right, I’ll send a 1-page agreement and we can start {{start_date}}.
